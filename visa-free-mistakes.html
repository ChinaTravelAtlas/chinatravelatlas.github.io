China Visa-Free Mistakes That Strand First-Timers

Visa-free and 240-hour transit look simple until an officer slides your form back. No data. No working Alipay. No easy way home.

What goes wrong if you do not prepare

  • Arrival card mismatch — passport number typos, blank hotel address, flight number errors. Incomplete entry while the line moves past you.
  • Wrong read of visa-free / 240-hour rules — ineligible port, wrong nationality assumptions, itinerary that is a stay not a transit.
  • No data on landing — airport Wi‑Fi stalls; maps, translation, and SMS verification die together.
  • Alipay / WeChat Pay fail on day one — foreign cards need setup that only works with home Wi‑Fi and working SMS before you fly.

Executable fixes before you board

  1. Confirm the exact visa-free or 240-hour rule for your passport, ports, and dates. If it does not fit, get the right visa.
  2. Fill arrival-card fields carefully. Keep hotel booking and onward ticket as PDF and paper.
  3. Install Alipay and WeChat on home Wi‑Fi; bind your card while SMS still works.
  4. Activate a mainland China eSIM (or confirmed roaming) so data and payments work on landing.
  5. Save hotel address in Chinese and English; carry modest cash for the first 24 hours.
