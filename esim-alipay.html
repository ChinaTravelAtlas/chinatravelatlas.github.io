No data and no Alipay is how the first afternoon falls apart

What goes wrong: airport Wi-Fi drops the moment you leave the terminal. Maps will not load, the ride you meant to book never confirms, and a foreign bank card is declined at the metro gate, a small restaurant, and the convenience store. Cash still works in some places, but you spend the first hours hunting an ATM and a shop that will take it, instead of getting to the hotel.

The fix: install a China-capable eSIM, or confirm your home plan will roam, while you still have internet at home. Then open Alipay and link a foreign card before you fly — identity check and card setup are much harder on a dead phone in the arrivals hall. WeChat Pay is the other common wallet if Alipay will not take your card. Do both steps the day before departure, not after you land.
