A blank arrival card is how the immigration line stops on you

What goes wrong: you reach the booth with an empty card, a nickname instead of the name in your passport, or no address for the first night. The officer sends you back to a side counter. The queue behind you keeps moving. You stand there rewriting the form while everyone else clears. A mismatch between the card and the passport page is the usual reason, not a rare one.

The fix: fill the arrival card on the plane, or the electronic version if the airline or the inspection point gives you one, before you join the booth line. Copy the surname and given names exactly as they appear in the passport, including the passport number. Put a real address for night one — hotel name and city is enough if you do not have a street number yet. Keep the card with your passport, not in the bag you already sent to the belt.
